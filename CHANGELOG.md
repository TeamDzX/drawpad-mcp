# Changelog

## 2.0.0
- First release, alongside DrawPad Pro.stl 2.0. Tools: `list_templates`,
  `create_from_template`, `create_custom` and `check_stl`. Each design comes
  with a watertight STL, an editable `.lsproj` project, a preview image, and
  its size, volume and filament weight. Universal binary (Apple silicon and
  Intel).
